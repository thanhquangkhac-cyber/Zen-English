Học Tiếng Anh Tinh Gọn

Bỏ qua lối mòn học thuật rườm rà. Dưới đây là 4 phương pháp tối giản, khoa học mang lại hiệu quả cao nhất cho người bận rộn.

01

Micro-learning

Tận dụng những khoảng thời gian trống 5-10 phút trong ngày để học từ vựng hoặc nghe podcast thay vì cố gắng học liên tục 2 tiếng mệt mỏi.

02

Contextual Input

03

Spaced Repetition

02

Hội Thoại Thực Tế

Tình huống: HSE Officer dẫn dắt toolbox talk buổi sáng với công nhân trước ca làm việc. Chủ đề: làm việc trên cao.

HSE Officer:Good morning, everyone. Before we start today's shift, let's take 10 minutes for our toolbox talk. Today's topic is "Working at Height Safety."
Workers:Good morning.
HSE Officer:Yesterday, I noticed two workers on the scaffolding without their safety harnesses clipped in. That's a serious unsafe act and could result in a fatal fall. Can anyone tell me — what are the three things you must check before working at height?
Worker A:Check the harness for damage, anchor point strength, and... the permit to work?
HSE Officer:Exactly right. You need a valid permit to work, a pre-use inspection of your harness, and confirmation that your anchor point is rated for fall arrest. Now — what do you do if you see a colleague not wearing their harness?
Worker B:We can use our stop work authority and tell them to stop.
HSE Officer:Perfect. Every single one of you has stop work authority. There is no pressure to continue working unsafely. Any questions before we start?
Worker A:What if the anchor point looks damaged?
HSE Officer:Report it immediately. Do not use it. Tag it out of service and inform your supervisor. Safety never comes second to productivity. Alright — stay safe, look out for each other, let's have a good day.

Tình huống: ISO 45001 auditor phỏng vấn HSE Manager trong buổi đánh giá chứng nhận. Chủ đề: quy trình nhận diện rủi ro.

Auditor:Good afternoon. I'm here to audit Clause 6.1 — Actions to address risks and opportunities. Can you walk me through your hazard identification process?
HSE Manager:Of course. We conduct hazard identification at three levels: during routine inspections, when planning new activities, and after any incident or near miss. The results feed into our risk register, which we review quarterly.
Auditor:How do you determine which risks require control actions?
HSE Manager:We use a risk matrix — 5×5 likelihood versus severity. Any risk rated "High" or "Very High" requires immediate corrective action and is escalated to the Operations Director. Medium risks are addressed within 30 days.
Auditor:Can you show me objective evidence of the most recent risk assessment?
HSE Manager:Yes, here is the risk register updated last month. You can see the hazard, the initial risk rating, the control measures applied, and the residual risk score. Each entry is signed off by the department manager.
Auditor:I see a nonconformance was raised in the last surveillance audit regarding worker participation in hazard identification. How was that closed out?
HSE Manager:We implemented a frontline safety observation program — workers submit observations via a mobile app or paper form. Participation is tracked monthly. I have the closure report and three months of observation data as evidence.
Auditor:Good. I'll review those records now.

Tình huống: HSE Officer báo cáo sự cố cho Ban Giám Đốc sau khi một công nhân bị thương nhẹ trong kho hàng.

HSE Officer:Good afternoon. I need to brief you on an incident that occurred at 9:15 this morning in the warehouse.
Director:Go ahead.
HSE Officer:A warehouse operator sustained a laceration to his left hand while cutting open a pallet wrap. The immediate cause was using a box cutter without a retractable blade guard. He received first aid on site and has been cleared to continue work — so this is classified as a first aid case, not a lost-time injury.
Director:What was the root cause?
HSE Officer:Our initial investigation identified two root causes: the correct retractable cutter was not available in that area, and the task was not covered in the JSA for warehouse duties.
Director:What are the corrective actions?
HSE Officer:We've taken three immediate actions: all non-guarded cutters have been removed from site, retractable cutters issued to all warehouse staff, and we're updating the JSA. The CAPA will be fully closed within 14 days. I'll circulate the lessons learned to all warehouse teams by Friday.
Director:Good. Make sure we share this across all three shifts so it doesn't happen again.

Tình huống: HSE Officer hướng dẫn an toàn cho nhà thầu mới vào công trường. Công việc: lắp đặt tủ điện.

HSE Officer:Welcome to our site. Before you begin any work, I need to walk you through our PPE requirements. These are mandatory — no exceptions.
Contractor:Understood. What do we need?
HSE Officer:For all areas: hard hat, safety glasses, high-visibility vest, and steel-toed boots. For your specific scope — electrical panel installation — you will additionally need insulated gloves rated for the voltage level and a face shield.
Contractor:We brought our own PPE. Is that acceptable?
HSE Officer:It can be, provided it meets our standards. I'll need to conduct a PPE inspection before work starts. Any damaged or non-compliant items must be replaced. We do have spare PPE on site if needed.
Contractor:What about during breaks — can we remove PPE in the work area?
HSE Officer:No. PPE must be worn at all times within the designated work zone. If I observe anyone without PPE in the work area, work will be stopped immediately.
Contractor:Clear. We'll comply fully.

Tình huống: ESG Manager thảo luận về mục tiêu giảm phát thải khí nhà kính với đối tác quốc tế trong cuộc họp online.

Partner:We'd like to understand your current GHG performance and reduction targets before we finalize the partnership agreement.
ESG Manager:Our 2023 baseline year shows total emissions of 4,200 metric tonnes CO2e. Scope 1 accounts for about 35% — primarily from diesel generators and company vehicles. Scope 2 is 60% — purchased grid electricity.
Partner:What are your reduction commitments?
ESG Manager:We have committed to a 40% absolute reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030. Key initiatives include transitioning to 100% renewable electricity through Power Purchase Agreements by 2026 and converting our fleet to EVs by 2028.
Partner:Are your targets validated by the Science Based Targets initiative?
ESG Manager:We submitted our targets for SBTi validation in Q1 this year. We expect approval within six months. Our methodology aligns with a 1.5-degree pathway.
Partner:How do you report your emissions? Do you follow the GHG Protocol?
ESG Manager:Yes. We use the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard with operational control boundary. Our emissions inventory is third-party verified annually. The 2023 verification statement is included in our sustainability report, which I'll share after this call.

Tình huống: Auditor phỏng vấn một nhân viên thông thường để kiểm tra mức độ nhận thức an toàn trong tổ chức.

Auditor:Hello. I'm conducting the internal audit today. Do you mind if I ask you a few questions about safety?
Worker:Sure, no problem.
Auditor:What is the OH&S policy of this company? Do you know it?
Worker:Yes. Our policy is to provide a safe and healthy workplace for all employees and contractors. We are committed to preventing injury, illness, and continual improvement of our safety performance.
Auditor:If you identified a hazard during your work today, what would you do?
Worker:I would stop and assess whether it's safe to continue. If it's a high risk, I would stop the work and report it to my supervisor. I would also log it in the safety observation system.
Auditor:What would you do if you were injured at work?
Worker:I would inform my supervisor immediately, seek first aid treatment, and complete an incident report. Even if it's minor — like a cut — I know I need to report it.
Auditor:Thank you. You've clearly been well-trained.

Tình huống: HSE Manager giải trình về việc đóng điểm không phù hợp (NC) với certifier trước khi nhận chứng nhận ISO.

Certifier:I'm reviewing the closure of NC-04. The finding was: "No evidence of competence evaluation for personnel performing safety-critical tasks." Can you present your closure evidence?
HSE Manager:Yes. Our root cause analysis identified that our competence framework did not specifically list safety-critical roles. We have since revised the framework and created a Safety-Critical Roles Register — 18 positions requiring verified competence before performing their tasks.
Certifier:Can you show me evidence of competence evaluation for current staff in those roles?
HSE Manager:Yes. Here is the competence matrix for all 18 roles. The highlighted column shows the evaluation date and the assessor's signature. For the six personnel who did not meet requirements at the time of the NC, they completed refresher training in March and were reassessed. All passed.
Certifier:I'll need to verify three records at random. May I see the training records for these employees?
HSE Manager:Certainly. Here are the signed training attendance records, the assessment results, and the updated certificates. All records are maintained in our documented information system.
Certifier:This is satisfactory. I'll mark NC-04 as closed. Well done.
